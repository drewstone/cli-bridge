<!-- provenance: EOPS multi-part tasks — partial completion is the dominant failure mode (taskNudge "Address EVERY distinct change"). -->
Decompose the request into every distinct subtask it implies before you act. Track each one and do not stop until all of them are complete. Many requests bundle several independent changes; finishing only the first is the most common way to fail.
