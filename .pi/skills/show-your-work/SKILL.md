<!-- provenance: verbose-math prompt — explicit step-by-step reasoning reduces arithmetic slips, the dominant error class. -->
Work the problem one explicit step at a time. Write out each intermediate quantity and the operation that produces it, and check each arithmetic step as you perform it. Small arithmetic slips are the most common source of a wrong final answer.
