# NorthStar Auto Group — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** trust-signal, local-pride
**Category:** Auto dealerships (4 locations)
**Channel:** Google + YouTube
**Budget tier:** $11k/wk (split across 4 GMs / geos)

## Voice description

Built on credibility, not aspiration. The copy sounds like the community it serves — not corporate, not flashy, but solid. "Your neighbors trust us" is the subtext of every line. Trust-signal means the copy leads with proof before promise: certifications, years in business, local roots, named staff. Local-pride means the geo is specific, not generic.

## Vocabulary

- Use: certified, trusted, local, your [city/neighborhood], [X] years, [staff name], service, straightforward, no-hassle, right price
- Named social proof: "Our service team…" not "our team"
- Specific geography: name the city, the street, the region when possible
- Avoid: luxury, premium, exclusive, game-changing, best deals (too generic), transformation, dream car (too aspirational)

## What trust-signal/local-pride means here

- Trust-signal = the copy doesn't ask you to trust it; it gives you reasons to.
- Local-pride = the reader recognizes themselves and their community in the copy.
- Never sounds national. Never sounds like a corporate campaign. Feels hand-signed.
- YouTube can carry a bit more warmth and story. Google Search is stripped-down, functional.

## Channel notes

- **Google Search:** Intent is high. Copy must match search language. Lead with the functional offer (model, service type, location) — trust signals are secondary.
- **YouTube:** Can build. Trust-signal through testimonial tone or visual credibility. Local reference early.

## Geo isolation — 4 dealerships, 4 GMs

Each variant set may need geo-targeted variants per location. Confirm with campaign brief whether:
- One universal creative (brand) with location extensions, or
- Separate copy per dealership with local name/address callouts

Do not mix dealership references across geo-targeted variants. Each GM approves only their location's creative.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into NorthStar Auto copy:

- **NOT Pelican Bay:** no aspirational-escape, no sensory luxury. Car buyers are making a practical decision even if it's also emotional. Pelican Bay register would ring false.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no irreverence, no edge, no challenge culture. NorthStar buyers want to feel respected, not tested.
- **NOT Helix Bio:** no clinical caution, no hedged claims. Auto copy can make direct claims backed by certifications.
- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** no literary pacing, no wry restraint. NorthStar is direct.
- **NOT SunwellCo / Tidepool:** warmth here is community-trust, not personal relationship or parental care.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

dream car, luxury experience, premium lifestyle, transform your commute, game-changing, best deals in town (unverifiable), exclusive offers (unless truly exclusive), see why we're #1 (unless certified)

## Claim discipline

Any ranked claim ("top-rated," "#1 in region") requires a verifiable citation. GMs will know if a claim is wrong — they live in the market.
