# NorthStar Auto Group — Approval Policy

**Approvers:** GM at each of 4 dealerships — all 4 must approve variants running in their market
**Channels:** Google + YouTube
**Budget:** $11k/wk (clarify split across 4 dealerships)
**Complexity:** 4 approvers, potentially 4 different revision requests. Coordinate carefully.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 variants (2 Google + 1 YouTube), trust-signal/local-pride register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD checks register; confirm local specificity is accurate (do not use generic "local dealership" copy — name real community anchors if possible)
3. **Route to all 4 GMs simultaneously** — same package to all 4; do not stagger
4. **Each GM approves for their market** — variants may need localization per dealership; if running market-specific versions, each GM approves only their market's variant
5. **Consolidate feedback** — if GMs give conflicting direction, escalate to agency CD; do not arbitrate
6. **Revision pass** — route revised version back to the requesting GM(s) only
7. **Launch** — all 4 GMs written approval before spend activates in their respective markets

---

## 4-GM coordination model

**If running identical variants across all 4 markets:**
All 4 GMs receive the same package; all 4 must approve. Any one GM can block their market.

**If running market-localized variants:**
Each GM receives and approves only their market's creative. Document which variant goes to which market.

Clarify with client which model applies this week before routing.

---

## What GMs care about

- Local accuracy — does this copy feel like it comes from this community, not a national chain?
- Inventory alignment — is the vehicle/offer featured actually available in that GM's lot?
- Trust signals — testimonials, service record, community presence. Does the ad earn trust or assert it?
- YouTube: Does the 15-second cut work? GMs will watch it.

---

## Submission format

Per GM:
- Label variants clearly with market name (not "Variant A" — use dealership location)
- Include offer/inventory note: "This variant assumes [model/offer] is available — confirm before routing"
- YouTube: include both the 15-second and 30-second cut if applicable, or note which length is being produced

---

## YouTube-specific notes

- 15-second non-skippable: every second is load-bearing. Trust signal in first 3 seconds.
- 30-second skippable: skip happens at 5 seconds. Hook must land by second 4.
- No jingles, no national-chain aesthetics. Looks local.

---

## What blocks launch

- [ ] All 4 GMs written approval (or per-market approval if localized)
- [ ] Inventory/offer accuracy confirmed per market
- [ ] Voice check passed — trust-signal/local-pride, no generic auto-dealer template copy
- [ ] YouTube variant confirmed skippable or non-skippable per campaign spec

## Budget note

$11k/wk across 4 dealerships and 2 channels. Confirm per-market and per-channel budget split before routing to GMs — they will ask.
