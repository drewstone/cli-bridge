# NorthStar Auto Group — Approval Workflow

**Approvers:** GM at each of 4 dealerships (separate approvals per geo)
**Channels:** Google + YouTube
**Budget:** $11k/wk across 4 locations

## Geo approval structure

Each GM approves only the creative for their dealership. There is no group-level approval. This means:

- If variants are geo-targeted: 4 separate approval paths, potentially 4 different timelines
- If variants are universal (brand-level with location extensions): confirm whether one GM leads or all 4 must sign

**Clarify with account lead before routing: universal creative or geo-targeted creative?**

## Routing (geo-targeted)

For each dealership:
1. Draft variants specific to that location (name, address, local reference)
2. Route to that dealership's GM individually
3. GM approves / requests changes
4. Do not mix approvals — GM at Location A does not approve Location B creative

## Routing (universal creative)

If the brief calls for one set of variants with location extensions:
1. Confirm which GM leads the approval process (or whether all 4 must sign)
2. Route to all 4 GMs simultaneously
3. Require all 4 to approve before any location goes live

## Submission format

Per variant, per geo (or universal):
- Headline (Google Search: max 30 chars each; YouTube: full)
- Description (Google: max 90 chars each; YouTube: full script or voiceover notes)
- CTA
- Location callout (address, city reference, dealership name)
- Any ranked or certified claim with source

## SLA estimate

Route by **Thursday AM**. GMs are operational — they review when convenient. A 24h buffer is tight but workable.

## Escalation

If a GM hasn't responded by Friday AM: flag to account lead who manages that dealership relationship. Do not launch without approval for that location.
