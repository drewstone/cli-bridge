# Bramble & Oak Coffee — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** literary, slow-burn
**Category:** Specialty coffee
**Channel:** Meta
**Budget tier:** $4k/wk (lowest — concepts must be achievable within this)

## Voice description

The copy earns its register. Deliberate word choice. Structural pacing. Restraint over enthusiasm — what's left out matters as much as what's in. Not pretentious: this is craft, not performance. A slight wryness lives underneath — the brand knows exactly what it is and doesn't need to prove it. The hook doesn't rush. The reader comes to the revelation, they're not hit with it.

## Vocabulary

- Use: early, quiet, ground, whole, slow, this cup, that hour, particular, small
- Specific nouns over general ones: "the kettle" not "hot water," "6am" not "morning"
- Literary devices: understatement, controlled repetition, the well-placed fragment
- Avoid: bold, discover, experience, premium, artisanal (cliché in this category), passion, crafted with love, small batch (overused)
- Never use: wake up and hustle, fuel your day, power through — that's ForgeFit register borrowed for coffee

## What literary/slow-burn means here

- Literary = the copy rewards a second read. Word choices are intentional, not interchangeable.
- Slow-burn = the copy doesn't lead with the loudest claim. It earns the reader's attention through specificity and pacing.
- Wry ≠ ironic distance. The brand cares about the coffee. The wryness is about not overselling it.
- Meta constraint: slow-burn must still stop the scroll. First line does the work; the rest is payoff.

## Critical constraint: Founder is also the writer

The solo founder writes their own copy. This means:
- They have strong, informed opinions on voice. They will push back on anything that doesn't sound like them.
- Treat this as a collaborative draft, not a final deliverable.
- Frame variants as options with rationale, not mandates.
- If a variant breaks a voice rule they set, they'll catch it — and it will cost relationship capital.

## Budget constraint

$4k/wk is the smallest budget. Do not propose concepts that require significant production (video, UGC casting, multi-format splits). Static or minimal-motion creative, strong copywriting, and the existing brand aesthetic are the tools.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into Bramble & Oak copy:

- **NOT Pelican Bay:** both are elevated in register, but Pelican Bay is sensory-aspirational and earnest. Bramble & Oak has restraint and a wry edge. "Escape" is not in B&O's vocabulary.
- **NOT SunwellCo:** no warm/plain-spoken DTC register. B&O doesn't talk to you like a friend — it talks like a letter.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no energy, challenge culture, or "fuel" framing. Coffee is not performance nutrition here.
- **NOT Tidepool / Helix Bio:** no warmth-for-safety or clinical register.
- **NOT Atlas Survey / NorthStar:** no functional/technical language, no ROI framing, no trust signals via certifications.
