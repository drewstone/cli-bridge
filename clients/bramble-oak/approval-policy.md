# Bramble & Oak Coffee — Approval Policy

**Approver:** Solo founder (also the brand's writer)
**Channel:** Meta
**Budget:** $4k/wk — smallest on the roster; every dollar is felt
**Dynamic:** The founder is a writer. They will have opinions about copy. This is a collaborative approval, not a rubber-stamp.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 Meta variants, literary/slow-burn register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD checks register; confirm no Pelican Bay contamination (both literary-adjacent; different pacing)
3. **Route to founder** — present as a conversation, not a delivery
4. **Founder reviews** — expect copy edits, not just approve/reject. The founder may rewrite lines.
5. **Incorporate founder edits exactly** — do not "clean up" or "harmonize" their edits back toward agency style. Their voice is the brand voice.
6. **Revised version** — route back only if agency changes were necessary (e.g., character limit compliance)
7. **Launch** — founder written approval; Meta campaigns activate

---

## The writer dynamic

The founder writes for the brand in their non-paid content. They will read agency copy with a writer's eye, not a marketer's eye. This means:

- **Rhythm and syntax matter as much as message.** A technically correct line they find clunky will be rewritten.
- **Present copy as a starting point** in your cover note, not as a finished artifact. This is not managing down expectations — it is respecting the collaboration.
- **If the founder rewrites a line, do not revert it.** Their edit is the approved version.
- **If the founder's edit breaks a character limit,** flag it as a platform constraint and ask how they'd like to handle it. Do not silently truncate.

---

## Submission format

Send founder:
- All 3 variants, labeled V1 / V2 / V3
- Brief note on the angle each variant is running (what emotional beat is it trying to land?)
- Frame the submission: "Here are three directions — take what's useful, push back on what isn't"
- Do not over-explain the creative rationale; they're a writer, not a client who needs the concept walked through

---

## What blocks launch

- [ ] Founder written approval
- [ ] Any founder copy edits incorporated exactly
- [ ] Character limit compliance verified after edits
- [ ] Voice check passed — literary/slow-burn, no Pelican Bay sensory luxury or SunwellCo warmth bleed

---

## Budget sensitivity note

At $4k/wk, this is not a client where you run 6 variants and let the algorithm sort it out. The 3 variants should be meaningfully distinct angles. Wasted spend on a weak variant is more visible here than on any other account.
