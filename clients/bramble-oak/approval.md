# Bramble & Oak Coffee — Approval Workflow

**Approver:** Solo founder (who is also the brand's writer)
**Channels:** Meta
**Budget:** $4k/wk

## Founder-as-writer dynamic

This is the highest-opinion approval chain in the roster. The founder writes Bramble & Oak's copy in other contexts — they have a refined and personal voice, and they will recognize when a variant doesn't sound like them. Treat this approval process as a collaborative review, not a one-way sign-off.

**Frame submissions as:** "Here are three angles — here's why each one fits the voice brief. Push back on any that miss."

## Routing

1. Draft 3 variants → internal voice check against `voice.md`
2. Submit with brief rationale per variant (1–2 lines on strategic angle)
3. Founder reviews — expect feedback, not just yes/no
4. Revise if needed — one round is likely
5. Founder gives final written approval
6. Do not publish to Meta Ads Manager without approval

## Submission format

Per variant:
- Headline / hook
- Primary text
- CTA
- 1–2 sentence rationale (why this angle fits B&O's voice)
- Flag any word choices that are borderline — better to surface them than have the founder discover them

## Budget constraint reminder

$4k/wk. Do not propose formats that require production the budget can't support. Static or minimal-motion, strong copy.

## SLA estimate

Route by **Thursday AM**. Build in one revision round. Founder is a solo operator — allow 24h response time.

## Escalation

If founder hasn't responded by Friday AM, a single follow-up is appropriate. Do not pressure — this is a relationship account.
