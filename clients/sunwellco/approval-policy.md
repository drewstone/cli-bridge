# SunwellCo — Approval Policy

**Approver:** Mara (sole sign-off, all variants)
**Channels:** Meta
**Budget:** $18k/wk
**Approval speed:** Mara is a single point — route with enough lead time that one delayed response doesn't miss the window.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 variants complete, voice-checked against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD confirms warm/plain-spoken register; no wellness-hype contamination
3. **Route to Mara** — send all 3 variants together, not one at a time
4. **Mara approves / requests revision** — one round of revision is standard; two rounds is a signal the brief was off
5. **Launch** — Meta campaign manager activates on Mara's written approval

---

## What Mara approves

Everything — copy, visual direction, audience targeting, budget allocation within the week. No sub-delegation. If Mara is unavailable, the variant waits. Do not route to anyone else.

---

## Submission format

Send Mara:
- All 3 variants in one document, clearly labeled V1 / V2 / V3
- Channel format per variant (feed, story, reel — if applicable)
- One-line rationale per variant (what angle and why)
- Plain-language notes on anything that might draw a question (new claim, new audience segment)

Do not send legal markup or internal process notes to Mara. Present clean creative.

---

## FTC / claim considerations

SunwellCo is a DTC supplement brand. FTC guidelines on supplement advertising apply:
- No disease claims without substantiation on file
- No "clinically proven" unless the specific formulation has been clinically tested
- Testimonials must reflect typical results; disclaim if atypical
- Before/after imagery requires disclaimer

If a draft contains a claim that is not on the current approved claims list, flag it internally before sending to Mara — not after.

---

## What blocks launch

- [ ] Mara's written approval (reply email or Slack confirmation)
- [ ] No unapproved efficacy claims
- [ ] Voice check passed — no wellness-hype register, no Helix Bio / Tidepool contamination

---

## Note on contamination risk

SunwellCo and Tidepool are both warm-register. SunwellCo and Helix Bio are both health-adjacent. Review `voice.md` contamination guards before every draft session.
