# SunwellCo — Style Guide

**Channel:** Meta (feed, story, reel)
**Voice:** Warm, plain-spoken — see `voice.md`
**Category:** DTC supplements

---

## Copy length by placement

| Placement | Primary text | Headline | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed (single image) | 80–125 chars before truncation | 25–40 chars | 30 chars |
| Story | Minimal on-screen text — 1 short phrase | N/A | N/A |
| Reel (video) | Caption: 1–2 sentences | N/A | N/A |
| Carousel | 60–80 chars per card | 25 chars per card | Optional |

---

## Tone in practice

- Sentence fragments are fine if they land with warmth
- Contractions always (it's, you're, we've)
- Second-person singular (you, your — not "our customers")
- Plain ingredient names over scientific names unless the scientific name is the differentiator
- Price or value mentioned conversationally, not as a shout

---

## Visual direction

- Pull from `[sunwellco]` Figma design token library
- **Palette:** Warm earth tones, natural textures, uncluttered backgrounds
- **Imagery:** Real people in natural contexts; ingredients in natural settings; no lab imagery (that's Helix Bio)
- **Typography:** Readable at small sizes; warm serif or humanist sans
- **No clinical aesthetics** — no white-coat, no test tubes, no data tables

---

## What this brand never looks like

- Helix Bio (clinical, data-heavy, regulatory-cautious)
- Tidepool (pediatric, family-safety framing)
- ForgeFit (irreverent, gym-bro energy)
- Any pharmaceutical or medical device aesthetic
