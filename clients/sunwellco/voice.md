# SunwellCo — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** warm, plain-spoken
**Category:** DTC supplements
**Channel:** Meta
**Budget tier:** $18k/wk

## Voice description

Talks like a knowledgeable friend, not a health authority. Short sentences. Everyday language. The copy trusts the reader — it shares, doesn't push. Optimistic without over-promising. Specific enough to be credible, simple enough that no one needs a dictionary.

## Vocabulary

- Use: feel, notice, actually, daily, you, real, simple, better, most people
- Numbers anchor credibility: "94% of customers…" not "most people…"
- Active verbs. Present tense by default.
- Sentence length: mostly short. Occasional medium. No complex subordinate clauses.

## What warm/plain-spoken means here

- Relational, not clinical. One reader, not a population.
- Accessible, not dumbed down. Reader is smart; copy just doesn't show off.
- Warmth lives in the relationship, not the word choices — no saccharine adjectives.
- Plain-spoken means concrete nouns and active verbs, not simplistic ideas.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into SunwellCo copy:

- **NOT Helix Bio:** zero clinical mechanism-of-action language, no peer-review citations, no "in a randomized study" framing. SunwellCo copy does not sound like a white paper.
- **NOT Pelican Bay:** no aspirational-escape framing ("imagine yourself…"), no luxury sensory description, no second-person fantasy. SunwellCo is grounded in daily life, not aspiration.
- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** no literary devices, no wry irony, no slow-burn structural pacing. SunwellCo is direct, not literary.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no challenge framing, no gym-bro register, no "this is not for everyone," no hard-edge energy.
- **NOT Tidepool:** no family-safety frame, no clinical-professional credentialing in copy.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

scientifically proven, cutting-edge, transformative, optimize your health, elevate your performance, where every moment matters, a ritual worth keeping, for those who demand more, the difference you can feel (too ForgeFit), backed by science (too Helix Bio)

## Claim discipline

Any efficacy claim needs a source on file before it appears in copy. Flag to Mara if a claim is new or unverified.
