# Atlas Survey — Approval Policy

**Approvers:** CMO (strategy) + Legal (compliance) — both required
**Channel:** LinkedIn
**Budget:** $9k/wk
**Process type:** Parallel review — route to CMO and legal at the same time; do not serialize

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 variants complete, dry/technical register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD checks register; confirm no Helix Bio contamination (both B2B; different hedging postures)
3. **Simultaneous route to CMO + legal** — send the same package to both at the same time
4. **CMO:** Reviews messaging strategy, competitive angle, audience fit
5. **Legal:** Reviews any claims about product capabilities, customer outcomes, or competitive comparisons
6. **Reconcile feedback** — if CMO and legal give conflicting direction, flag to agency CD; do not arbitrate between them
7. **Revised version** — route back to the party who requested changes for sign-off
8. **Launch** — written approval from both CMO and legal

---

## What each approver cares about

**CMO:**
- Is this message on-strategy for the quarter?
- Does it reach the right ICP (survey ops, research ops, enterprise analytics buyers)?
- Does the competitive angle reflect current market positioning?

**Legal:**
- Any claim about uptime, accuracy, or data security needs either a verified stat or removal
- No customer outcome claims without an approved case study or NDA carve-out
- No competitor names unless the legal team has explicitly cleared comparative advertising for this campaign

---

## Submission format

Send both parties:
- All 3 variants, labeled V1 / V2 / V3
- One-line audience/goal rationale per variant
- Internal note flagging any claim that may require legal scrutiny (do not leave legal to discover this)

**Do not send legal-markup drafts to CMO and vice versa.** Each receives a clean version with relevant flagging for their role.

---

## What blocks launch

- [ ] CMO written approval
- [ ] Legal written approval
- [ ] No product capability claims without verified source
- [ ] No competitive references unless pre-cleared
- [ ] Voice check passed — dry/technical, not sales-forward, no Helix Bio or SunwellCo register bleed
