# Atlas Survey — Style Guide

**Channel:** LinkedIn
**Voice:** Dry, technical — see `voice.md`
**Category:** B2B SaaS

---

## Copy length

| Element | Limit | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Intro text | 600 chars | 200–300 chars — LinkedIn B2B audience skims |
| Headline | 150 chars | 60–80 chars |
| Description | 100 chars | 50–70 chars |
| CTA | Platform-provided | "Learn More" or "Request Demo" |

---

## Tone in practice

- Lead with what the product does, not what the buyer will feel
- Specificity over generality: "3-minute deploy" beats "quick setup"
- No exclamation points
- Technical vocabulary is appropriate; assume the reader knows the domain
- If citing a metric, cite it precisely — "94% survey completion rate in 2024 benchmark" not "industry-leading completion rates"

---

## Visual direction

- Pull from `[atlas-survey]` Figma design token library
- **Palette:** Clean blues and grays; data visualization colors if showing product UI
- **Imagery:** Product UI screenshots, dashboard views, data output examples — not stock photos of people in meetings
- **Typography:** Technical sans-serif; data-density-friendly
- **No lifestyle imagery** — this is not a consumer product

---

## What this brand never looks like

- Helix Bio (regulated biotech caution — Atlas Survey is confident, not hedged)
- SunwellCo (warm consumer; Atlas Survey is cold technical)
- ForgeFit / Pelican Bay / Bramble & Oak (irreverence, aspiration, literary pacing — none of these)
- Any brand that sounds like it's talking to an individual rather than a buying committee
