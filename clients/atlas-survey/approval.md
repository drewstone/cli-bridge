# Atlas Survey — Approval Workflow

**Approvers:** Legal + CMO (sequential — legal first, then CMO)
**Timing constraint:** Legal review can take 1–2 business days
**Channels:** LinkedIn

## Routing

1. Draft all 3 variants → internal review
2. Route to **Legal first** — legal has blocking authority. CMO does not review until legal clears.
3. Legal returns redlines or approval → incorporate any required changes
4. Route revised copy to **CMO** for final sign-off
5. Do not submit to LinkedIn Campaign Manager before CMO written approval

## Why legal goes first

Legal review on LinkedIn B2B SaaS copy typically flags: competitive comparisons, unverifiable performance claims, superlatives ("most," "best," "only"). CMO review on top of unapproved legal copy wastes everyone's time if legal strikes the same lines.

## Submission format to legal

- Variant label (A / B / C)
- Full copy (headline + body + CTA)
- Annotation of any claim that could be interpreted as a competitive comparison or performance claim
- Source for any statistic used

## Submission format to CMO

- Legal-cleared copy (mark which lines were modified per legal)
- Brief rationale for each variant's strategic angle
- Recommended launch order (if testing)

## SLA estimate

Legal: 1–2 days. CMO: same-day if legal clears cleanly.
Route to legal by **EOD Thursday** to hit Friday with a tight window. If brief is ready earlier, route Wednesday to allow a revision round.
