# Atlas Survey — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** dry, technical
**Category:** B2B SaaS
**Channel:** LinkedIn
**Budget tier:** $9k/wk

## Voice description

Precision over charm. Speaks to operators and decision-makers who have seen every buzzword and are immune to them. Copy earns credibility by being specific, measurable, and direct. No enthusiasm inflation. The drier the copy, the more trustworthy it sounds to this audience. Dry ≠ boring — it means every word pulls its weight.

## Vocabulary

- Use: data, response rate, sample size, signal, confidence, integrate, export, API, workflow, segment
- Numbers preferred over adjectives: "3-minute setup" not "quick setup"
- B2B register: ROI, time-to-value, compliance, scalability
- Third-person for product references is fine: "Atlas Survey delivers…"
- Avoid: journey, unlock, empower, seamless, powerful, robust, game-changing

## What dry/technical means here

- Dry = no manufactured enthusiasm. The product is good; let the proof carry it.
- Technical = specificity. Name the feature, name the output, name the integration.
- No emotional hooks in headlines. Leads with the functional benefit.
- LinkedIn audience can smell fluff at 20 words. Every sentence must justify itself.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into Atlas Survey copy:

- **NOT Helix Bio:** avoid claim-substantiation language and regulatory hedging — Atlas Survey is commercial B2B SaaS, not regulated science. "Studies show" and "evidence-based" belong in Helix Bio.
- **NOT Pelican Bay:** no sensory description, no aspirational escape. B2B copy is grounded in operational outcomes, not feelings.
- **NOT SunwellCo:** no warmth, no "you'll feel the difference" register. B2B buyers don't want to be talked to like a friend.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no irreverence, no edge, no challenge-culture. A CMO buying survey tools is not a gym.
- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** no literary pacing, no wry voice, no slow reveals. Atlas Survey copy is front-loaded.
- **NOT Tidepool:** no parental warmth, no safety framing.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

unlock insights, empower your team, transform your business, seamless experience, game-changing, robust platform, journey, powerful tool, where data meets decisions, feel confident in your data

## LinkedIn-specific constraints

- No engagement-bait. No questions designed to fish for comments.
- No listicles disguised as insight ("5 reasons your surveys are failing").
- Thought-leadership tone requires actual data points — not generic assertions.
- Legal will flag any competitive comparison claims. Avoid.
