# ForgeFit Apparel — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** irreverent, gym-bro
**Category:** Apparel (fitness/lifestyle)
**Channel:** Meta + TikTok
**Budget tier:** $22k/wk (highest — concepts can be ambitious)

## Voice description

Talks to people who take training seriously and take themselves not too seriously. Punchy. Self-aware. Occasionally absurdist. The copy has earned the right to swagger because the product backs it up. It's not trying to be liked by everyone — that's the point. Short sentences. Blunt truths. No hand-holding.

## Vocabulary

- Use: built, earned, lift, hit, grind, done, real, heavy, soft (as pejorative), show up, move
- Sentence fragments are features, not bugs.
- Profanity: tasteful edge OK, not gratuitous. Founder's call on anything spicy.
- Numbers work when brutal: "5am. No excuses."
- Avoid: journey, empower, inspire, community, celebrate, mindful, wellness, self-care

## What irreverent/gym-bro means here

- Irreverent = the brand doesn't take marketing language seriously. It mocks the category while being in it.
- Gym-bro ≠ toxic — it's the culture of people who prioritize the physical and respect others who do.
- Punchy > clever. A line that lands hard beats one that's witty.
- TikTok copy especially: first line must hit immediately. No warm-up.

## Channel notes

- **Meta:** Can afford slightly more setup. Still front-loaded.
- **TikTok:** Line 1 is the whole ad. Everything else is follow-through. Hook must be complete in 5 words or fewer.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into ForgeFit copy:

- **NOT SunwellCo:** no warmth, no relational register, no "you'll feel better" framing. ForgeFit doesn't comfort.
- **NOT Tidepool:** no family-safety warmth, no clinical authority. ForgeFit is not safe-for-all — it's selective.
- **NOT Pelican Bay:** no aspirational-escape, no sensory luxury description, no "slow down and breathe." ForgeFit is acceleration.
- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** no literary pacing, no wry intellectualism. ForgeFit doesn't wink at itself via poetry.
- **NOT Helix Bio / Atlas Survey:** no technical language, no citations, no mechanism-of-action. ForgeFit proves itself in reps, not whitepapers.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

your wellness journey, empower your best self, celebrate every win, mindful movement, feel your best, community of supporters, self-care is strength, crafted with intention (too Bramble & Oak), where every detail matters

## Budget note

At $22k/wk this is the highest-spend client. Concepts can include video, UGC, and multi-format splits. Don't undershoot ambition to match a lower-tier creative skeleton.
