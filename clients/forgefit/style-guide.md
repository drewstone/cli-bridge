# ForgeFit Apparel — Style Guide

**Channels:** Meta + TikTok
**Voice:** Irreverent, gym-bro — see `voice.md`
**Category:** Performance apparel, fitness culture

---

## Copy length

| Placement | Primary text | Headline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta feed | 80–125 chars | 25–40 chars | Punch on line 1 |
| Meta story | Minimal text | Short phrase | Visual/energy first |
| TikTok caption | 100–150 chars | N/A | Hashtags: 3–5 max |
| TikTok script | Hook 1–2s / Body 10s / CTA 3s | N/A | See TikTok section |

---

## Tone in practice

- Short sentences. Fragments. No hedging.
- Profanity: use contextually; check platform policies per placement (TikTok stricter than Meta)
- Self-aware irreverence — the brand is in on the joke, not making fun of the audience
- Specific gym culture references land; generic "get fit" language doesn't
- No inspirational quotes. No "journey." No "transform your body" (every brand says this).

---

## Visual direction

- Pull from `[forgefit]` Figma design token library
- **Palette:** High contrast; black, white, and one aggressive accent (confirm current season color with founder)
- **Imagery:** Real athletes in real gym contexts; sweat, weight, effort visible — not sanitized product shots
- **Typography:** Bold, compressed, high-contrast — readable at speed on a phone screen
- **Energy:** If you have to ask whether it's high-energy enough, it isn't

---

## TikTok production notes

- **Hook (0:00–0:02):** Visual action or text overlay that creates a reason to keep watching. Not a logo. Not a product shot.
- **Body (0:02–0:12):** The claim, demonstration, or joke — whichever angle the variant runs
- **CTA (0:12–0:15):** One clear action; link in bio is standard
- **Audio:** Sound-on is assumed. Music selection matters — align with current TikTok culture, not a generic beat
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16. No black bars. No watermarks.

---

## What this brand never looks like

- Polished, aspirational resort/lifestyle aesthetic (that's Pelican Bay)
- Warm and relational (that's SunwellCo or Tidepool)
- Dry and technical (that's Atlas Survey)
- Safe. Cautious. Hedged. (That's Helix Bio and the opposite of ForgeFit)
