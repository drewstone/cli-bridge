# ForgeFit Apparel — Approval Policy

**Approver:** Founder (sole sign-off)
**Channels:** Meta + TikTok
**Budget:** $22k/wk — highest on the roster
**Hard constraint:** Founder reviews after 7pm PT only. Do not send before 7pm PT. Do not follow up before 7pm PT.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 variants complete (2 Meta + 1 TikTok), irreverent/gym-bro register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD checks register; confirm energy level is right — ForgeFit punches harder than any other client on the roster
3. **Hold until 7pm PT** — draft sits staged and ready; send at 7:00pm PT sharp (or as close as possible)
4. **Founder reviews** — fast approver historically; expect same-evening or next-morning response
5. **Revision** — founder may redline copy directly (they have a strong voice); incorporate exactly as given
6. **Launch** — founder written approval; activate Meta and TikTok campaigns

---

## The 7pm PT rule

The founder has stated they review after 7pm PT. This is not a preference — it is a boundary. Sending before 7pm creates friction and risks a slower response. Schedule the send.

If a deadline genuinely requires earlier review (production emergency, platform policy issue), contact the agency CD to decide whether to escalate — do not unilaterally break the window.

---

## What the founder cares about

- Does this sound like ForgeFit, not a generic fitness brand?
- Is the irreverence earned or try-hard? (The founder will know the difference immediately)
- TikTok: Is this built for native TikTok behavior, or does it look like a Meta ad repurposed?
- Meta: High-energy scroll-stopper — does V1 stop the thumb?
- Budget: At $22k/wk this is the highest-stakes approval on the roster. The founder takes it seriously even if the tone is casual.

---

## Submission format

Send founder:
- All 3 variants labeled by channel (Meta V1, Meta V2, TikTok V1)
- TikTok variant: include script structure (hook / body / CTA) plus visual/audio direction notes
- Meta variants: include visual concept note
- Keep the cover note brief and in ForgeFit voice — the founder is not reading agency formality

---

## TikTok-specific notes

TikTok variants must be scripted for native behavior:
- Hook in first 1–2 seconds (text overlay or action)
- Sound-on assumption
- Creator-style authenticity over polished production
- No watermarks from other platforms
- Script format: `[0:00–0:02 hook] [0:02–0:12 body] [0:12–0:15 CTA]`
