## Isolation rules

1. Open one client's `voice.md` at a time. Do not have two clients' copy open simultaneously.
2. If a phrase would work equally well for two clients, it is wrong for both. Revise.
3. No cross-client copy reference. Do not lift phrases from another client's variants/.
4. Approval chains are client-specific. Do not route copy through the wrong approver.
5. After drafting, run a contamination check: read the variant aloud and ask "which client is this?" If the answer is ambiguous, it fails.
