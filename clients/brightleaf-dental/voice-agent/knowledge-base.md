- **In-network with Delta Dental** — submit claims directly; patient pays copay at visit
- Accepts most major PPO plans — verify before visit
- **Not in-network with:** Medicaid, Medicare (for dental), HMO plans
- **Uninsured:** Brightleaf membership plan available — annual fee covers cleanings, exams, X-rays, plus discounts on procedures. Exact pricing: Maria has the sheet.

### Same-Day Emergency Policy
- Dr. Patel holds emergency slots open every weekday until 10:00 AM
- After 10:00 AM, unused emergency slots may be released to non-emergency bookings
- **Emergency criteria:** Tooth pain, broken/chipped tooth, lost filling or crown, swelling, abscess, dental trauma
- **Not emergency:** Routine cleaning scheduling, whitening questions, mild sensitivity that has lasted weeks
- **Severe cases (911 first):** Facial swelling with fever, difficulty breathing, trauma with loss of consciousness, uncontrolled bleeding — direct caller to ER first, then schedule follow-up

## Common Caller Questions and Exact Answers

**"Do you take Delta Dental?"**
"Yes, we're in-network with Delta Dental. We file the claim for you, and you just pay your copay at the visit. Maria can verify your specific benefits before you come in."

**"How much is a cleaning without insurance?"**
"A routine cleaning and exam without insurance is [price — get from Maria]. We also have a membership plan that covers two cleanings, exams, and X-rays for the year at a flat rate. I can have Maria send you the details."

**"Can I get a same-day appointment?"**
"It depends on the reason. If you're in pain, have a broken tooth, or lost a filling, Dr. Patel holds emergency slots same day. Let me check what's available. If it's for a routine cleaning, our next opening is usually within a week or two."

**"Do you do root canals / wisdom teeth?"**
"Dr. Patel does root canals and simple extractions here in the office. If a wisdom tooth is impacted or the case is complex, we refer to an oral surgeon we trust — we can handle the referral and follow-up care."

**"What if I have to cancel?"**
"We ask for 24 hours notice. Cancellations with less than 24 hours may be subject to a [amount — get from Lisa] fee. If you need to reschedule, just call us and we'll find you a new time."

**"Do you offer sedation?"**
"We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for anxiety. For IV sedation or general anesthesia, we refer to our oral surgery partner. Let Dr. Patel know at your visit if you're nervous — we have options."

**"What COVID protocols do you still follow?"**
"We follow CDC and ADA guidelines. Our instruments are sterilized, surfaces disinfected between patients, and our team is up to date on health protocols. Masks are optional in the waiting area."

**"Can my child come alone?"**
"Patients under 18 need to be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. If a guardian can't attend, we need a signed consent form in advance."

## Competitor Context (If Asked)

If a caller mentions another local dental practice, do not speak negatively about them. Use this framing:
- "We're a small, patient-focused practice. Dr. Patel sees every patient personally. If you didn't feel comfortable there, we'd love the chance to earn your trust."
- Never say: "They're more expensive," "They overbook," or "Dr. Patel is better."

## Prohibited Responses

- Do not diagnose over the phone. Always defer to Dr. Patel and an in-person exam.
- Do not quote exact prices for procedures without confirming with Maria.
- Do not promise insurance coverage before verification.
- Do not say "I don't know" without offering to find out or transfer.
- Do not book appointments during lunch hours (12:30–1:30 PM).
- Do not schedule on weekends or holidays.
