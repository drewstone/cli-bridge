## Line-by-line legal review

Legal does not review copy holistically — they initial each line. Structure submissions accordingly:

**Submission format for legal:**

```
Variant A — LinkedIn Ad

[Line 1] [_____]
[Line 2] [_____]
[Line 3] [_____]
[CTA]    [_____]

Notes: [flag any claim that may require legal attention]
```

Leave initials blank for legal to fill. If a line is modified, track the change (original → revised).

## What legal flags

- Any efficacy claim not qualified with "may," "suggests," "in studies," etc.
- Competitive comparisons
- Terms that imply FDA status the company doesn't have (treat, cure, prevent, approved for)
- "Proven" or "clinically proven" without verified source
- Any claim that could be read as a health claim under FTC standards
