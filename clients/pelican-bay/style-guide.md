# Pelican Bay Resort — Style Guide

**Channels:** Meta + Google
**Voice:** Aspirational, sensory — see `voice.md`
**Category:** Hospitality / destination resort

---

## Copy length

| Placement | Primary text | Headline | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta feed | 80–125 chars before truncation | 25–40 chars | Lead with sensory detail |
| Meta story | Minimal text | Short phrase only | Visual does the work |
| Google search | N/A | 30 chars × 3 headlines | Responsive search ad — keyword-aware |
| Google display | 90 chars | 25 chars | Sensory hook in headline |

---

## Tone in practice

- Open with a sense impression, not a feature ("Salt air and open water" over "Oceanfront resort")
- Specificity signals authenticity — name real places, real experiences on property
- Sentence rhythm matters: read copy aloud; it should sound like an invitation
- "You" is singular and unhurried — this is not a mass offer
- Avoid urgency mechanics that undercut aspiration ("limited time," "act now," "only X rooms left")

---

## Visual direction

- Pull from `[pelican-bay]` Figma design token library
- **Palette:** Ocean blues, warm whites, natural textures; golden hour tones for Meta
- **Imagery:** Actual property photography only — no stock resort imagery
- **Typography:** Elegant serif for headlines; airy spacing; never compressed
- **No discount callouts** in the visual layer (price may live in body copy if owner approves)

---

## Channel-specific notes

**Meta:** Emotion first. The first 1.5 seconds of the visual decides everything. Sound-off context — no copy dependency on audio.

**Google:** Intent-based. The searcher is already looking for a destination — speak to what makes Pelican Bay the answer. Sensory language works in search headlines; it differentiates from the generic "Book Now" competitors.

---

## What this brand never looks like

- Generic resort template ("Experience paradise. Book today.")
- Deal-hunter framing (percentage off, flash sale mechanics)
- Bramble & Oak (reflective/literary pacing — Pelican Bay evokes, it doesn't ruminate)
- Any other roster client's visual palette
