# Pelican Bay Resort — Approval Policy

**Approver:** Owner (sole sign-off, all variants and both channels)
**Channels:** Meta + Google
**Budget:** $14k/wk
**Critical note:** Owner approval is slow. Route today. Friday is not recoverable if you wait until Thursday.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 variants complete (2 Meta + 1 Google), aspirational/sensory register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **Internal review** — agency CD checks register; confirm no Bramble & Oak contamination (both literary-adjacent; different registers)
3. **Route to owner** — send all variants together; do not drip one at a time
4. **Owner reviews** — owner makes final calls on all creative, targeting, and spend. No sub-delegation documented.
5. **Revision (likely)** — owner approval often takes 1–2 rounds. Build this into the timeline.
6. **Launch** — owner written approval before any spend activates on either channel

---

## Timing reality

Owner approval is the longest pole in this client's process. The sequence:
- **Route EOD Wednesday** — the variant must be in the owner's inbox before they go to bed
- **Expect Thursday response** — if no response by noon Thursday, follow up
- **Friday launch** is achievable only if the owner responds Thursday with no major revisions

If the owner requests significant changes Thursday afternoon, Friday launch is at risk. Flag this immediately to agency CD if it happens.

---

## What the owner cares about

- Sensory accuracy — does this copy actually evoke Pelican Bay, or is it generic resort copy?
- Guest experience alignment — does the ad promise an experience the property delivers?
- Tone — aspirational but not over-promising. The owner knows what the property can and can't do.
- Brand cohesion between Meta and Google variants (they know both channels will run simultaneously)

---

## Submission format

Send owner:
- All 3 variants clearly labeled with channel (Meta V1, Meta V2, Google V1)
- Visual concept note per variant (2–3 sentences on the imagery direction)
- Clean presentation — no internal process notes or legal markup

## What blocks launch

- [ ] Owner written approval (reply email is sufficient)
- [ ] Both channels cleared (owner approves Meta and Google variants separately if they differ)
- [ ] Voice check passed — aspirational/sensory, no generic resort copy, no Bramble & Oak register bleed

## Note on contamination risk

Pelican Bay and Bramble & Oak are both in the literary-adjacent register. Pelican Bay is sensory luxury; Bramble & Oak is slow-burn literary. The contamination direction to guard against: Bramble & Oak's reflective pacing bleeding into Pelican Bay. Pelican Bay copy should evoke — not ruminate. See `voice.md` contamination guards.
