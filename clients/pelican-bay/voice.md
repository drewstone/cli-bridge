# Pelican Bay Resort — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** aspirational, sensory
**Category:** Resort / hospitality
**Channel:** Meta + Google
**Budget tier:** $14k/wk

## Voice description

Puts the reader inside the experience before they've booked. Sensory-first: what they'll see, hear, smell, feel. The copy earns aspiration by being specific — not "luxurious" but "salt air and a ceiling fan turning slow." Second-person present tense is the default register. Pelican Bay doesn't sell a trip; it sells a state of being.

## Vocabulary

- Use: light, salt, linen, slow, still, warm, quiet, unhurried, horizon, morning, texture-specific adjectives
- Second-person present: "You're watching the water…" not "Guests enjoy…"
- Sensory verbs: feel, hear, watch, taste, breathe
- Restraint in superlatives — earned descriptors only, not "world-class"
- Avoid: amenities, facilities, features, packages, value, affordable, convenient

## What aspirational/sensory means here

- Aspirational = the copy creates a world the reader wants to enter, not a list of benefits.
- Sensory = grounded in physical detail. Abstract aspiration without sensory specificity is empty.
- Tempo is slower than DTC. The copy can breathe. A three-word sentence followed by a longer one is intentional pacing.
- The reader should feel slightly envious of themselves-in-the-future.

## Channel notes

- **Meta:** Visual copy. Lead with the sensory image; the caption completes it. Shorter is better for feed.
- **Google:** Search intent is high. Balance aspirational language with enough functional signal that search copy converts.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into Pelican Bay copy:

- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** both are elevated in register but literary/wry ≠ sensory-aspirational. Pelican Bay has no irony, no wink. It's earnest about beauty.
- **NOT SunwellCo:** no plain-spoken register, no "real language" DTC warmth. Pelican Bay earns distance from the everyday.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no energy, no challenge, no hustle-adjacent language. Pelican Bay is the antidote to ForgeFit's world.
- **NOT Tidepool:** no family-safety framing (unless brief calls for family segment). No clinical authority.
- **NOT Atlas Survey / Helix Bio:** no B2B technical language. No ROI, no compliance, no data.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

world-class amenities, unbeatable value, book now for the deal, convenient location, features include, package pricing, something for everyone, an experience you won't forget (too generic)

## Claim discipline

Any "award-winning," "best of," or comparative claim must be verified against actual recognition before use. Owner reviews all copy — they will catch inaccuracies.
