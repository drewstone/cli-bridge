# Tidepool Pediatric Therapy — Brand Voice

**Voice tags:** warm, COPPA-aware
**Category:** Pediatric therapy services
**Channel:** Meta
**Budget tier:** $5k/wk

## Voice description

Speaks to parents, not children. The warmth is earned through competence and trust, not softness — parents of children in therapy are making high-stakes decisions. The tone is professional authority delivered with empathy. It does not oversell outcomes. It positions Tidepool as the informed, steady presence that parents need to feel they're making the right call.

## Vocabulary

- Use: your child, families, qualified, experienced, evidence-based, support, progress, individualized, together, outcome, specialist, schedule
- Parental emotional register: "You want the best for them." "It's hard to know where to start."
- Clinical credibility without clinical coldness: name the modality (ABA, speech-language, OT) when relevant
- Avoid: heal, cure, fix your child, transform, guaranteed outcomes, miraculous, better than, leading (unless verifiable)

## What warm/COPPA-aware means here

- Warm = parental trust, not consumer friendliness. This is SunwellCo's warmth inverted: less "friend," more "the right professional."
- COPPA-aware = Meta ads for Tidepool target adults (parents) only. No copy that directly addresses children. No creative depicting child identifiers that could be used for targeting.
- Do not use copy that implies the child has a problem — frame around what's possible, not what's wrong.

## CRITICAL DISTINCTION from SunwellCo

Both SunwellCo and Tidepool are warm. The contamination risk is real.
- **SunwellCo warmth** = personal, relational, "like a friend who knows supplements."
- **Tidepool warmth** = competent authority + parental empathy. It carries clinical weight. It does not feel casual.
- A Tidepool line that sounds like a supplement ad is a compliance and brand failure simultaneously.

## COPPA and ad targeting rules

- Audience targeting must be adults (18+). Do not target by child-related interests that could proxy for child audiences.
- No creative that shows identifiable children without written consent on file.
- Do not collect or reference child data in any ad creative or landing-page connection.
- Clinical director reviews: flag any claim about treatment outcomes or therapeutic efficacy.

## Contamination guards

Do NOT let these voices bleed into Tidepool copy:

- **NOT SunwellCo:** no DTC supplement warmth, no "you'll feel better" register, no casual friend tone.
- **NOT Helix Bio:** Tidepool is warm; it is not cold clinical/regulatory. It does not cite studies in ad copy.
- **NOT ForgeFit:** no challenge culture, no edge, no irreverence — this audience is in a vulnerable moment.
- **NOT Pelican Bay:** no aspirational-escape. Parents of children in therapy are not fantasizing; they are deciding.
- **NOT Bramble & Oak:** no literary pacing, no wryness. This is not the moment for restraint as an aesthetic.

## Banned phrases (flag if any appear)

fix your child, heal them, guaranteed results, your child's problem, broken, struggling (as the lead), transform your child, we cure, better than other therapies, leading provider (unless verified), our method works every time

## Clinical director review scope

The clinical director reviews for:
1. Outcome claims (any implication of guaranteed or typical progress)
2. Diagnostic framing (copy that names or implies a diagnosis)
3. Modality claims (describing a therapy's efficacy beyond what's clinically warranted)
Flag these before routing to clinical director so the review is efficient.
