# Tidepool Pediatric Therapy — Approval Workflow

**Approver:** Clinical director
**Channels:** Meta
**Budget:** $5k/wk

## Clinical director review scope

The clinical director reviews for professional accuracy and ethical positioning — not just brand fit. Expect scrutiny on:

1. **Outcome claims** — any implication of guaranteed or typical progress ("your child will improve," "see results in X weeks")
2. **Diagnostic framing** — copy that names or implies a diagnosis in a way that could mislead
3. **Modality claims** — describing a therapy's efficacy beyond what the clinical evidence supports
4. **Targeting compliance** — clinical director may flag copy that inadvertently addresses children rather than parents

**Flag these categories when submitting so the review is efficient.** Don't make the clinical director hunt for potential issues.

## COPPA flag in submission

Include a COPPA confirmation with each submission:

```
COPPA check:
[ ] Copy addresses adults (parents) only — no direct child address
[ ] No child identifiers used for targeting
[ ] No copy that could imply child data collection
[ ] Visual direction (if applicable) does not include identifiable children without consent on file
```

## Routing

1. Draft 3 variants → internal voice check + COPPA check
2. Annotate any lines in categories 1–4 above
3. Route to clinical director with annotations
4. Clinical director reviews and returns with approval or redlines
5. Incorporate redlines → re-route if changes are material
6. Clinical director final written approval
7. Do not publish to Meta without clinical director sign-off

## Submission format

Per variant:
- Headline / hook
- Primary text (annotated for clinical flags)
- CTA
- COPPA checklist
- Any claim about outcomes or modalities with notes on the basis for the claim

## SLA estimate

Route by **Thursday AM**. Clinical directors are busy — allow one business day. If submitted Thursday AM, expect response Thursday EOD or Friday AM. Tight, but workable.

## Escalation

If no response by Friday AM, contact clinical director directly. Do not publish without sign-off. Pediatric therapy copy has professional liability exposure — no exceptions.
