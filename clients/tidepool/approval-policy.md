# Tidepool Pediatric Therapy — Approval Policy

**Approver:** Clinical Director
**Channel:** Meta
**Budget:** $5k/wk
**Regulatory constraint:** COPPA compliance is non-negotiable. Every variant must pass COPPA review before routing to clinical director.

---

## Approval chain

1. **Creative draft** — 3 Meta variants, warm/COPPA-aware register confirmed against `voice.md`
2. **COPPA pre-check** — agency CD reviews every variant against COPPA checklist below before it leaves the folder
3. **Clinical Director review** — routes all 3 variants; clinical director reviews for clinical accuracy, appropriate messaging, and brand fit
4. **Revision pass** — clinical director may request changes to how services are described or how outcomes are framed; incorporate exactly
5. **Launch** — clinical director written approval; Meta campaigns activate

---

## COPPA pre-check (must pass before routing to clinical director)

COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) applies because this is a pediatric therapy practice advertising on Meta. Meta's own policies on advertising to parents about children's health services add a second layer.

Before any variant leaves this folder, confirm:

- [ ] No collection or reference to personal data from children under 13 in the ad itself
- [ ] No targeting parameters that could inadvertently reach children (confirm with media buyer; audience must be parents/guardians, not minors)
- [ ] No imagery of children without explicit documented releases
- [ ] Ad creative does not imply diagnosis or treatment of a specific condition without clinical director sign-off on the exact language
- [ ] No before/after framing for therapy outcomes (FTC and clinical ethics concern)
- [ ] Testimonials: if any parent testimonial is used, it must be real, documented, and FTC-disclaimed

If any item is unchecked, the variant does not route to clinical director — it goes back to draft.

---

## What the clinical director cares about

- Clinical accuracy: are services described correctly?
- Appropriate framing: therapy should not be marketed like a consumer product
- Warmth without false hope: the copy can be warm; it cannot imply guaranteed outcomes
- Community trust: Tidepool operates in a specific community; the clinical director knows what language resonates vs. what feels foreign
- Protecting vulnerable families: parents seeking pediatric therapy are often under stress; predatory or manipulative ad mechanics are a hard no

Send clinical director:
- All 3 variants, clearly labeled
- COPPA checklist confirmation note (tell them it has been reviewed — they want to know)
- Flag any language describing clinical services; these are highest scrutiny for the clinical director

## Note on contamination risk

Tidepool and SunwellCo are both in the warm register. The contamination to guard against: SunwellCo's earthy consumer warmth bleeding into Tidepool's clinical warmth. Tidepool speaks to parents making care decisions for their children — the warmth is protective, not consumptive. See `voice.md` contamination guards.
