## What happened

Your TikTok draft was reviewed on May 13 against FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR §255.5)
and TikTok's Branded Content Policy. Three required disclosure elements are missing. All three
must be corrected before the post can go live or payment can release.

These are legal requirements, not creative notes. A published post missing any of these is
an FTC compliance issue for both you and the brand. We had a formal complaint in March for
exactly this reason.

---

## Three required changes

### 1. Enable TikTok Branded Content toggle — BEFORE you publish

In TikTok → Creator Tools → Branded Content: enable the toggle for this post.
This adds the platform-rendered "Paid Partnership" label at the top of the video.

**This cannot be added after the post is live. If you publish without it, the post must
be deleted and re-published with the toggle on.**

Your current draft has no evidence of the toggle being staged.

---

### 2. On-screen disclosure within the first 3 seconds

Within the first 3 seconds of the video, one of the following must be present:

**Option A — spoken aloud** (clearly audible, not under loud music):
> "This is a paid partnership with Tailspin Outfitters."

**Option B — on-screen text overlay** (contrasting color, stays on screen ≥3 seconds):
> `#ad` or `Paid Partnership with Tailspin Outfitters`

The current draft has on-screen text reading **"the only shell I trust"** appearing at
approximately 5 seconds. This is an endorsement claim, not a disclosure. It does not
satisfy this requirement on either dimension (wrong content, wrong timing).

---

### 3. `#ad` in the caption before any hashtag stack

The caption must contain `#ad` or `#sponsored`. It must appear before any other content —
not buried in a hashtag stack at the end.

✅ Correct: `#ad — I've been living in the Ridgeline Shell every morning this week...`
❌ Wrong: `Obsessed with this shell, lifesaver on the alpine traverse 🏔️ link in bio #outdoors #hiking #ad`

Your current caption — *"obsessed with this rain shell, lifesaver on the alpine traverse
last weekend 🏔️ link in bio"* — has no disclosure language anywhere.
