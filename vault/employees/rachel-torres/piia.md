This Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **May 1, 2026**, between **Tangle Tools, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and **Rachel Torres** ("Employee").

In consideration of Employee's employment with the Company, the compensation paid therefor, and the Company's provision of Proprietary Information (defined below), the parties agree as follows:

---

## 1. Proprietary Information

### 1.1 Definition

"**Proprietary Information**" means any information or data, in any form (written, oral, electronic, or otherwise), that relates to the actual or anticipated business, research, or development of the Company, its affiliates, or its clients, that has or could have commercial value, and that is designated as confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Proprietary Information includes but is not limited to:

(a) trade secrets (as defined under the California Uniform Trade Secrets Act, Cal. Civ. Code §§ 3426–3426.11 (2024), and the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1836 (2024));
(b) technical data, research, product plans, algorithms, software code, databases, and systems architecture;
(c) business plans, financial projections, customer and prospect lists, pricing, margins, and competitive analyses;
(d) personnel data and compensation information;
(e) information obtained from third parties under obligations of confidentiality.

### 1.2 Exclusions

Proprietary Information does not include information that:

(a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement by Employee;
(b) was rightfully known to Employee before disclosure by the Company, as evidenced by contemporaneous written records;
(c) is rightfully received by Employee from a third party without restriction; or
(d) Employee independently develops without use of or reference to the Company's Proprietary Information.

---

## 2. Obligations of Confidentiality

During employment and for **five (5) years** following separation (and indefinitely for information constituting a trade secret under applicable law), Employee agrees to:

(a) hold all Proprietary Information in strict confidence;
(b) not disclose Proprietary Information to any third party without the Company's prior written consent;
(c) use Proprietary Information solely in furtherance of Employee's duties at the Company;
(d) limit access to Proprietary Information to persons within the Company who have a legitimate need to know.

### 2.1 Return / Destruction

Upon separation or upon request, Employee will promptly return or, at the Company's direction, destroy all Proprietary Information (including copies, notes, and derivative works), and will provide written certification of such return or destruction signed by Employee within five (5) business days.

### 2.2 Prior Employers' Confidential Information

Employee represents that employment with the Company does not require Employee to breach any obligation to a former employer, and Employee will not bring to or use at the Company any proprietary or confidential information of any former employer.

---

## 3. Assignment of Inventions

### 3.1 Assignment

Employee agrees to promptly disclose to the Company and hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company all right, title, and interest in and to any and all **Inventions** (defined below) that Employee makes, conceives, develops, or reduces to practice, solely or jointly, during the period of employment with the Company.

"**Inventions**" means discoveries, developments, concepts, designs, ideas, know-how, improvements, inventions, trade secrets, and works of authorship, whether or not patentable or registrable under copyright or similar laws, which Employee works on during employment.

### 3.2 California Section 2870 Carveout — REQUIRED NOTICE

**NOTICE PURSUANT TO CAL. LAB. CODE § 2872 (2024):**

The assignment in Section 3.1 **does NOT apply** to any Invention that qualifies fully under **Cal. Lab. Code § 2870 (2024)**, which provides:

> *Any provision in an employment agreement which provides that an employee shall assign, or offer to assign, any of his or her rights in an invention to his or her employer shall not apply to an invention that the employee developed entirely on his or her own time without using the employer's equipment, supplies, facilities, or trade secret information except for those inventions that either:*
> *(1) Relate at the time of conception or reduction to practice of the invention to the employer's business, or actual or demonstrably anticipated research or development of the employer; or*
> *(2) Result from any work performed by the employee for the employer.*

Employee should list on **Exhibit A** (Prior Inventions Schedule) any inventions made prior to employment that Employee wishes to exclude from assignment. If Exhibit A is blank or not attached, Employee represents there are no such inventions.

### 3.3 Works Made for Hire

To the extent any Invention constitutes a "work made for hire" under 17 U.S.C. § 101 (2024), it is owned by the Company from creation. To the extent it does not qualify, Employee assigns all rights to the Company under Section 3.1.

### 3.4 Moral Rights Waiver

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Employee waives and agrees not to assert any "moral rights" or similar rights in any assigned Invention.

### 3.5 License Fallback

If any Invention is not fully assignable to the Company due to applicable law (e.g., Section 2870 carveout), Employee grants the Company a royalty-free, irrevocable, perpetual, worldwide license to make, use, sell, sublicense, and modify such Invention, to the extent permitted by law.

### 3.6 Assistance and Cooperation

Employee agrees to execute such documents, provide such assistance, and take such further steps as the Company reasonably requests to evidence, perfect, protect, or enforce the Company's rights in any assigned Invention, during and after employment.

## 4. Non-Solicitation

### 4.1 No California Non-Compete

No non-competition covenant is contained in this Agreement. Any such covenant would be void under Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 16600 (2024).

### 4.2 No Solicitation of Employees

During employment, Employee agrees not to solicit, recruit, or encourage any Company employee to leave the Company.

> **NOTE:** Post-employment employee non-solicitation covenants are generally void in California under Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 16600 (2024) and Edwards v. Arthur Andersen LLP, 44 Cal. 4th 937 (2008). This Agreement does not impose any post-employment employee non-solicit. Confirm with California employment counsel before adding any post-employment restriction.

---

## 5. Defend Trade Secrets Act — Immunity Notice

**NOTICE REQUIRED BY 18 U.S.C. § 1833(b) (2024):**

An individual shall not be held criminally or civilly liable under any federal or state trade secret law for the disclosure of a trade secret that:
(A) is made in confidence to a federal, state, or local government official, or to an attorney, solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law; or
(B) is made in a complaint or other document filed in a lawsuit or other proceeding, if such filing is made under seal.

An individual who files a lawsuit for retaliation by an employer for reporting a suspected violation of law may disclose the trade secret to the attorney of the individual and use the trade secret information in the court proceeding, if the individual files any document containing the trade secret under seal; and does not disclose the trade secret, except pursuant to court order.

## 6. No Conflicting Obligations

Employee represents that: (a) Employee is not a party to any agreement that conflicts with this Agreement or Employee's employment; (b) Employee will not use at the Company any confidential information of any third party without authorization; and (c) Employee will promptly notify the Company if Employee becomes aware of any such conflict.

## 7. Injunctive Relief

Employee acknowledges that any breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company for which monetary damages alone would be an inadequate remedy. Accordingly, the Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in addition to all other remedies available at law, without the requirement to post bond. Nothing herein limits the Company's right to seek all remedies available.

**8.3 Forum.** Any dispute arising hereunder shall be resolved in the state or federal courts located in San Francisco County, California, and each party consents to such jurisdiction.

**8.4 Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

**8.5 Entire Agreement; Amendments.** This Agreement, together with the Offer Letter and the Equity Award Documents, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties on the subject matter hereof. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

**8.6 Counterparts; Electronic Signatures.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which shall constitute an original.
