## $100,000 ISO Annual Limit — IMPORTANT

Under 26 U.S.C. § 422(d) (2024), the aggregate fair market value (determined at grant) of shares for which ISOs first become exercisable in any calendar year may not exceed **$100,000**. The excess is automatically treated as an NSO.

**At the 1-year cliff (2027), 12,500 shares become exercisable.** At the exercise price of $[FMV_PER_SHARE]:
- If FMV ≤ **$8.00/share**: all 12,500 shares qualify as ISOs (12,500 × $8.00 = $100,000)
- If FMV > **$8.00/share**: the portion exceeding $100,000 in value automatically converts to NSO

The monthly tranches (months 13–48) each vest ~1,042 shares; at $8.00/share = ~$8,336/month, well under the annual $100k limit.

**Action required:** Confirm with your tax advisor whether ISO treatment is fully available at your current FMV before the grant date.

## Tax Treatment Summary (Employee)

| Event | ISO Treatment |
|-------|--------------|
| Grant | No income recognized |
| Exercise | No regular income tax; spread (FMV - Exercise Price) is an **AMT preference item** [26 U.S.C. § 56(b)(3), 2024] |
| Sale (qualifying) | **Long-term capital gain** if held > 2 years from Grant Date AND > 1 year from Exercise Date |
| Sale (disqualifying) | Spread at exercise treated as ordinary income (W-2); any additional gain is capital gain |

> A "disqualifying disposition" occurs if Employee sells within 2 years of grant or 1 year of exercise. The Company is required to report disqualifying dispositions.
