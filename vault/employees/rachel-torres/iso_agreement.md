This Stock Option Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into between **Tangle Tools, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the Grantee identified in the Stock Option Grant Notice (the "Grant Notice"), which is incorporated herein by reference.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given in the Grant Notice or the Company's 2026 Equity Incentive Plan (the "Plan").

---

## 1. Grant of Option

The Company grants to Grantee, as of the Grant Date set forth in the Grant Notice, an option (the "Option") to purchase the number of shares of Common Stock set forth in the Grant Notice (the "Shares"), at the Exercise Price per Share set forth in the Grant Notice, on the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Grant Notice, and the Plan. In the event of any conflict among these documents, the Plan controls, except that no conflict will be resolved by reducing Grantee's rights beyond what the Plan minimum requires.

**ISO Intent.** It is intended that the Option qualify as an Incentive Stock Option to the fullest extent permitted under 26 U.S.C. § 422 (2024). To the extent the Option does not qualify as an ISO (whether due to the $100,000 limit under 26 U.S.C. § 422(d) (2024), failure to satisfy a statutory requirement, or otherwise), it is a Non-Qualified Stock Option (NSO) with respect to those non-qualifying Shares. Neither the Company nor any of its officers, directors, employees, or advisors makes any representation or warranty that the Option will qualify as an ISO; Grantee is responsible for confirming ISO eligibility with Grantee's own tax advisor.

---

## 2. Vesting and Exercisability

**2.1 Vesting Schedule.** The Option vests and becomes exercisable according to the schedule in the Grant Notice, subject to Grantee's continuous service with the Company through each vesting date.

**2.2 No Acceleration.** Unless otherwise expressly approved in writing by the Board, unvested portions of the Option do not accelerate upon any event, including but not limited to a change of control, termination of employment, or any other event.

**2.3 No Right to Continued Employment.** Nothing in this Agreement grants any right to continued employment. Employment remains at-will [Cal. Lab. Code § 2922, 2024].

---

## 3. Exercise of Option

**3.1 Manner of Exercise.** Grantee may exercise vested portions of the Option by delivering to the Company: (a) a written or electronic notice specifying the number of Shares to be purchased; (b) payment of the aggregate Exercise Price by any method permitted under the Plan (cash, check, cashless net exercise, or other method approved by the Company); and (c) any withholding tax owed (for NSO exercises and disqualifying ISO dispositions).

**3.2 Minimum Exercise.** Grantee may not exercise the Option for fewer than 10 Shares (or the remaining vested balance if fewer than 10 Shares are unvested/unexercised), unless the Company waives this requirement in writing.

**3.3 Compliance with Law.** The Option may not be exercised if the issuance of Shares would violate any applicable law, rule, or regulation. The Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to register or qualify the Shares as necessary.

---

## 4. Expiration and Post-Termination Exercise

**4.1 Expiration.** The Option expires on the Expiration Date set forth in the Grant Notice, unless earlier terminated under this Section 4 or the Plan.

**4.2 Termination Without Cause or Voluntary Resignation.** If Grantee's continuous service terminates for any reason other than death, disability, or cause, Grantee may exercise the vested portion of the Option within **ninety (90) days** after the termination date (but not beyond the Expiration Date). Exercise after 90 days converts ISO status to NSO.

**4.3 Death.** If Grantee dies while in continuous service, the estate or beneficiary may exercise the vested portion within **twelve (12) months** after death (but not beyond the Expiration Date), as permitted by 26 U.S.C. § 422(b)(5) (2024).

**4.4 Disability.** If Grantee's service terminates due to total and permanent disability (as defined in 26 U.S.C. § 22(e)(3) (2024)), Grantee may exercise the vested portion within **twelve (12) months** after termination (but not beyond the Expiration Date), as permitted by 26 U.S.C. § 422(b)(6) (2024).

**4.5 Termination for Cause.** If the Company terminates Grantee's service for Cause (as defined in the Plan), the Option (both vested and unvested) terminates immediately upon termination and may not be exercised.

**4.6 Unvested Shares.** Unvested portions of the Option that have not vested as of the termination date are forfeited immediately upon termination, without payment or consideration.

## 5. ISO Holding Period Requirements

To obtain favorable ISO tax treatment (long-term capital gain on sale), Grantee must hold Shares acquired on exercise for:
- More than **2 years** from the Grant Date; AND
- More than **1 year** from the exercise date [26 U.S.C. § 422(a)(1), 2024]

A sale within these holding periods is a "disqualifying disposition" — the spread at exercise is taxed as ordinary income (reported on W-2), and Grantee must notify the Company within 30 days of such a disposition [26 U.S.C. § 422(b)(1), (e), 2024].

**AMT Warning.** Exercise of an ISO creates an AMT preference item equal to the spread (FMV at exercise minus Exercise Price) [26 U.S.C. § 56(b)(3), 2024]. Grantee should consult a tax advisor before exercising.

## 6. Non-Transferability

The Option is non-transferable, except by will or the laws of descent and distribution. During Grantee's lifetime, only Grantee may exercise the Option. Any purported transfer in violation of this Section is void.

---

## 7. Adjustments

In the event of a stock split, reverse split, stock dividend, recapitalization, reorganization, merger, or similar event affecting the Company's Common Stock, the Board may adjust the number of Shares subject to the Option, the Exercise Price, or both, in accordance with the Plan, to prevent dilution or enlargement of Grantee's rights.

---

## 8. Change of Control

In the event of a merger, acquisition, sale of all or substantially all assets, or other change of control transaction (as defined in the Plan), the Board may, in its sole discretion: (a) assume or substitute the Option; (b) accelerate vesting; (c) provide for a cash-out; or (d) allow the Option to terminate if not exercised prior to close. The Board is not required to treat the Option in the same manner as other awards. Grantee has no guaranteed rights in a change of control.

---

## 9. Tax Matters

**9.1 Tax Withholding (NSO and Disqualifying ISO Dispositions).** For any exercise or disposition that results in ordinary income, the Company shall withhold all applicable federal, state, and local income taxes and employment taxes. Grantee authorizes the Company to satisfy withholding from Grantee's paycheck, by requiring payment at exercise, or by reducing the number of Shares issued.

**9.2 No Tax Advice.** The Company makes no representation regarding the tax consequences of this Option. Grantee should consult an independent tax advisor before grant, exercise, and sale.

**9.3 Section 409A.** The Option is intended to be exempt from 26 U.S.C. § 409A (2024) as a stock right granted at FMV. The Agreement shall be interpreted consistent with this intent. The Company does not warrant that the Option will in fact be exempt, and Grantee remains solely responsible for any 409A taxes.
