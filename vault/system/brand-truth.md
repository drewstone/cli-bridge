# Brand Truth — E2E Skincare

**Confirmed truth (2026-05-17):** Co-founder is a dermatologist whose clinical practice is exclusively post-cancer patients. The product line was built first for skin recovering from chemotherapy, radiation, and hormonal therapy — the most compromised skin in clinical practice. These formulas cleared that clinical tolerance bar before any lifestyle skincare test.

**What this means:**
- The formulas were not designed for aspirational skin — they were designed for skin under stress
- The 55-70-year-old repeat buyer is not a mismatch with this truth; she is the intended beneficiary of it
- "Same actives as Tata Harper at half the price" is NOT the brand truth — it names a competitor as the reference point and abandons the only defensible ground

## Banned positioning frames (confirmed by founder)
- "Elevated / premium / approachable / luxury" — adjectives any competitor can use
- Price comparison to Tata Harper or any other brand
- 25-year-old model in soft daylight / glass-skin retouching (current ads: wrong audience)
- Manifesto / hero film / minimalist beige aesthetic

## Locked audience
**Primary:** Women 55-70, repeat buyers, AOV $62, already in funnel.
**Aspirational (NOT this brief):** Younger cohort migration requires explicit named brief, separate budget, kill criterion.

## Performance baseline (current ads, do not defend these numbers)
- CTR: 0.7%
- CPA: $89
- AOV: $62
- Creative: 25-year-old model, white robe — audience mismatch confirmed
