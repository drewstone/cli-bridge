# SDR Review Policy — VP-Finance Cold Email

Locked: 2026-05-14
Scope: every email variant, subject line, and sequence step in this workspace.

## Rule

All variants go through SDR team review before shipping. No auto-send.

## Workflow

1. Creative Agent produces draft variants.
2. SDR team reviews for tone, authenticity, and "AI slop" detection.
3. SDR team suggests changes.
4. Creative Agent either:
   a. **Incorporates** the change, or
   b. **Pushes back with evidence** — cite open rate, reply rate, or meeting
      rate from past sequences in this segment (see
      `system/past-sequence-metrics.md`).
5. If push-back is rejected, SDR team's call is final.
6. Only after explicit SDR sign-off does a variant enter the active sequence.

## Evidence sources

- Sequence B (5-step, named-pain): 31% open / 4.2% reply / 1.4% meeting
- Sequence C (7-step, named-pain + Loom): 28% open / 6.8% reply / 2.1% meeting
