## STEP 1 — Name the pain (Day 1)

### Subject variants:
- AR days at {{company.name}}
- 60 days on receivables
- {{company.name}} invoicing

### Body:

Hi {{contact.firstname}},

Most GCs your size are carrying 70+ DSO on commercial work. The holdup
usually isn't the payer — it's the approval chain between field tickets
and the GL.

{{company.name}}'s revenue and headcount suggest that chain is manual
right now. If it's costing you more than one FTE's time per month,
it's worth looking at.

Open to seeing how this works?

—[Sender]

## STEP 3 — Specific metric + soft CTA (Day 7)

### Subject variants:
- AR days at {{company.name}}
- 47 days vs. 78
- The approval bottleneck

### Body:

Hi {{contact.firstname}},

Quick math: if {{company.name}} runs $40M+ in annual revenue and DSO
is 75, you've got roughly $8M tied up in AR at any given time.

The GCs we work with tend to find that 30–40% of that delay is
internal — approval routing, duplicate entry, missing PO references
on field tickets.

We automate the stretch between "field ticket submitted" and "invoice
approved in the GL." Works with Sage Intacct, Foundation, and Viewpoint.

If that gap costs you a day a week in finance labor, should I send
over a short breakdown?

—[Sender]
