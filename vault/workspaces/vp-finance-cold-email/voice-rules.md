# Voice Rules — Invoice Automation (B2B SaaS, Construction)

Source: Notion voice doc at notion.so/co/voice
Locked: 2026-05-14
Applies to: all outbound email, paid ads, and brand copy for this workspace.

## Tone

Direct, never desperate. Name the financial pain explicitly.

## Banned words

leverage, synergy, journey, unlock, supercharge, exciting, thrilled,
seamless, robust, cutting-edge, innovative, empower, streamline

## Structural rules

- Never open with a compliment ("Loved your recent…", "Congrats on…").
- Never ask for 15 minutes or any specific time-block CTA.
- Never use "I hope this finds you well" or equivalent throat-clearing.
- CTA must be low-commitment and specific to the prospect's situation.
- Proof points must reference concrete metrics (DSO days, headcount, revenue)
  — no vague claims.

## CAN-SPAM + state footers

Every step must carry:
- **Standard footer:** company name, physical address, unsubscribe link.
- **CA / CO / VA footer:** adds "commercial email" notice, reply STOP option,
  privacy policy link, state of incorporation.

See sequence draft for full footer copy. Route both blocks through legal
review before activation.
