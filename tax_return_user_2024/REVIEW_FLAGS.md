# 2024 Tax Return — User Session — Review Flags / Audit-Defense File

## Critical open items (must resolve before filing)

### 1. Entity conflict — Spider Webb Tech Ltd
**Issue:** The same entity is described as both a W-2 payer (U.S. wages) and a CFC (foreign corporation generating GILTI). A foreign corporation generally does not issue U.S. Form W-2.
**What I need:** Clarify whether Spider Webb Tech Ltd is (a) a domestic entity that paid $0 management fee wages and is unrelated to the CFC, (b) a foreign corporation reclassified as domestic for payroll, or (c) a mislabeling in the facts. Also need ownership percentage (10% U.S. shareholder / 50% CFC test) to confirm Form 5471 filing category and GILTI inclusion eligibility.
**Risk if unresolved:** Form 5471 may be omitted or incorrectly filed; GILTI inclusion may be overstated or mischaracterized; W-2/Schedule C/K-1 classification may be wrong.

### 2. Real-estate professional hours
**Issue:** Ashworth Holdings LLC states "qualified real estate professional" on the K-1, but §469(c)(7) requires the individual taxpayer to materially participate and spend >750 hours and >50% of personal services in real property trades.
**What I need:** Contemporaneous log of hours by activity and property for taxpayer (and spouse if MFJ), plus grouping election status.
**Risk if unresolved:** The ($42,000) / ($84,000) rental loss may be recharacterized as passive and suspended under §469.

### 3. Charitable stock appraisal
**Issue:** $200,000 noncash charitable contribution requires a qualified appraisal and Form 8283 (noncash > $5,000).
**What I need:** Appraisal report, date of contribution, donor's adjusted basis in the stock, holding period, and whether the donee is a public charity.
**Risk if unresolved:** Contribution deduction may be disallowed or capped at basis; §6662 substantial understatement exposure.

### 4. QOZ investment basis / deferral election
**Issue:** $150,000 qualified opportunity zone investment requires tracing of deferred gain and Form 8997.
**What I need:** Date of QOZ investment, amount of gain deferred (if any), QOF EIN, and whether the investment was made with cash or with a gain rollover.
**Risk if unresolved:** Deferral / exclusion benefits may be lost; form may be incomplete.

### 5. Foreign financial asset reporting
**Issue:** Foreign accounts exceed $10,000 aggregate (FBAR) and likely exceed $100,000 year-end / $150,000 any-time threshold for Form 8938 (MFJ).
**What I need:** Highest balances during the year, interest/dividend income earned in accounts, whether any signature authority accounts exist, and FinCEN 114 filing history.
**Risk if unresolved:** Non-willful FBAR penalty up to $10,000 per account; Form 8938 penalty $10,000+.

### 6. California GILTI / §250 conformity
**Issue:** California does not conform to all federal international provisions. Need to verify whether the GILTI inclusion net of §250 deduction is treated as foreign-source business income or Subpart F for California, and whether California allows a §250 analogue or foreign tax credit.
**What I need:** California FTB source guidance for GILTI and §250; taxpayer's California-sourced income allocation.
