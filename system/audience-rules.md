# Audience Rules

## Buyer We Have (Primary)
- Women 55–70
- Sensitive, compromised, or reactive skin (often post-menopausal changes, rosacea, or barrier damage)
- Skeptical of beauty marketing; often burned by "clean" or "gentle" products that still irritate
- High reorder rate once converted (this is the repeat-buyer cohort)
- Willing to pay for efficacy but allergic to being overcharged for packaging/aspiration
- Responds to clinical credibility and age-recognition casting, not youth aspiration

## Buyer We Wish We Had (Secondary)
- Women 25–35
- "Prestige skincare" purchasers
- Currently targeted by existing hero creative (25-year-old model, glass-skin retouching)
- **Rule:** No creative targets this audience without an explicit strategic decision, budget, kill criteria, and graduation criteria documented in a proposal.

## Cost of Guessing Wrong
- If we continue creating for the 25-year-old aspirational buyer: CTR stays ~0.7%, CPA stays ~$89, AOV stays $62, and we burn budget reaching buyers who do not reorder.
- If we create for the 55–70 buyer: we match creative to the cohort that already reorders, improving CAC efficiency and LTV/CAC ratio.
