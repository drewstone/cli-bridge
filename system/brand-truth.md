# Brand Truth

**Primary (uncopyable):** Co-founder is a dermatologist who exclusively treats post-cancer patients. She built this skincare line for them first because their compromised skin could not tolerate existing products.

**Implication:** The line carries medical-practice credibility, extreme gentleness, and barrier-repair efficacy as table stakes. It was born from clinical necessity, not a market gap analysis.

**Secondary (supporting):** Same active ingredients as luxury competitors at roughly half the price. This is a pricing/value claim, not the emotional core. Use it as a rational close, not a lead.

**What this is not:** A formulation story about unique actives. The actives are not unique. The origin is.
