# Learned Style Rules

## Do
- Lead with the clinical origin story: dermatologist, post-cancer patients, built from necessity
- Cast real women 55–70; no glass-skin retouching, no smoothing, no youth aspiration
- Show actual skin texture; normalize aging
- Use founder/clinical voice when she speaks: direct, unpolished, factual, no script polish
- Make single claims per asset
- Use price transparency as a secondary weapon ("same actives, half the price")
- Write to channel mechanics: scroll-stop frame one, sound-off, native pacing

## Don't
- Use banned adjectives as load-bearing claims: elevated, premium, approachable, luxury, innovative, disruptive, fun, engaging, passionate, world-class, best-in-class, cutting-edge
- Cast 25-year-old models for primary-audience work
- Use agency-grade transitions, color grading, or music beds
- Write manifesto copy, aspirational lifestyle vignettes, or "founder on a couch holding the product"
- Name competitors directly in paid assets (reference "luxury serums" or "$200+ products" generally)
- Adapt the same idea across channels by reformatting; each channel earns its own concept
- Propose rebrands or refreshes without a named strategic reason tied to a business change
