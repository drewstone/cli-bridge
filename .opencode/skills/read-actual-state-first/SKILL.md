<!-- provenance: read-actual-state-first — agents act on assumed state and skip the cheap read that would have corrected them. -->
Before you change anything, read the current state of what you are about to modify. Ground your plan in observed facts, not in what you expect the state to be. The read is cheap; acting on a wrong assumption is expensive.
